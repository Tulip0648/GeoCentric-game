- base mode: 4-5 clues, roughly covering different directions. 6 or 10 guesses and 3 hints. (maybe difficulty levels?)
        - daily puzzle
        - practice puzzles
    - clue: fixed length no distance hint
    - 3 different modes: top 50 / 100 / 350 (or 500) cities by most famous cities (if possible?).
    - limited to India for now.
    - a mode for World capitals?
    - 1 hint reveal distance of 1 clue of choice.

- use Mercator Projection of Earth and then use Latitude and Longitude to calculate angles for now. So that it gives exact visual alignment with Google Maps.

- Data set: 
    - [city_name, lat, long, INDIA/WORLD, {country_name}/{state/UT}, <sort_rank>]. (looks good?)
    - Top N Cities in India (50, 100, 350, 500)
    - All World Capitals.

- Clue finding Algorithm: 
    - input : set of correct city (lat, long).
    - output: set of 5 clues (relative positions).
 
    - divide directions into 8 equal segments.
    - filter non-empty segments. wrt the correct city.
    - if > 5 segments available, choose any 5 segments uniformly at random. 
    - if < 5 segments available, choose all available segments. And then the remaining to be choosen randomly until 5 selected.
    - if == 5 segments available, choose all available segments.
    - for each segment choice, choose a city uniformly at random from that segment. if a city is chosen more than once, then choose another city uniformly at random from that segment.
    - keep doing until 5 distinct cities are chosen.
