    How to Play GeoCentric

    A mystery city is hidden in the center of the compass circle.

    • Arrows: Point directly towards surrounding reference cities based on their true geographic bearing.
    • Reference Cities: Displayed at the tip of each arrow with their state.
    • Hints: You have 2 hints per round. Each hint adds an extra reference city to the compass.
    • Wrong Guesses: Incorrect guesses are automatically plotted onto the compass to help you triangulate.
    • Goal: Guess the central mystery city within 6 attempts.
